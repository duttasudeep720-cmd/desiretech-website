Common Amazon seller problems

Questions Indian Amazon sellers ask us most

Practical starting points for common issues. These are general observations, not a diagnosis of your specific account — every account is different.

Why are my Amazon listings not getting enough visibility?

Likely causes

Weak or missing keywords in the title and bullets, incomplete listing content, low stock, uncompetitive pricing, or a category/attribute mismatch.

What to check

Search relevant customer terms and see where your listing appears, review your title/bullet content, and confirm stock and Buy Box status.

Corrective actions

Rework titles and bullets around real search terms, complete missing attributes, and ensure images and content meet Amazon's guidelines.

When support helps

If visibility hasn't improved after basic content fixes, or you have many listings to review, an outside review can help spot what's being missed.

My Amazon sales have dropped — what should I check?

Likely causes

Stock-outs, a lost Buy Box, price changes by competitors, listing suppression, seasonal demand shifts, or an account-health notification.

What to check

Review inventory levels, Buy Box status, recent account-health notices, and how your pricing compares to similar listings.

Corrective actions

Restock where needed, review pricing, resolve any flagged listing or account issues, and check that ad campaigns are still active and funded.

When support helps

When the cause isn't obvious from the dashboard, or several factors seem to be involved at once.

Why are my Amazon Ads spending without enough sales?

Likely causes

Broad or irrelevant keyword targeting, a listing that doesn't convert once clicked, high competition for the keywords used, or budgets spread too thin.

What to check

Review the search-term report for irrelevant clicks, check your listing's conversion elements (images, price, reviews, content), and review campaign structure.

Corrective actions

Add negative keywords, tighten targeting, improve listing content, and reallocate budget toward keywords that are actually converting.

When support helps

Ongoing campaign monitoring and optimisation is easier to sustain with dedicated attention, especially across multiple SKUs.

How do I improve my Amazon product listings?

Likely causes

Thin descriptions, missing attributes, low-quality images, or content that doesn't answer common customer questions.

What to check

Compare your listing against well-performing listings in the same category, and check completeness of every content field.

Corrective actions

Rewrite titles and bullets with clear, relevant information, complete all attributes, and coordinate updated images where needed.

When support helps

When you have a large catalog and need consistent content quality across many SKUs.

What should I do if my Amazon account has an account-health issue?

Likely causes

Policy violations, customer complaints, late shipments, authenticity concerns, or documentation requests from Amazon.

What to check

Read the exact notification carefully, check the Account Health dashboard, and gather any documentation Amazon has asked for.

Corrective actions

Address the root cause, prepare a clear response or Plan of Action, and submit through the correct Seller Central process.

When support helps

Reviewing warnings and preparing documentation with an outside perspective can help — though Amazon's decision cannot be guaranteed.

How can I manage a large Amazon catalog efficiently?

Likely causes

Manual, one-by-one listing updates don't scale well once a catalog grows past a few dozen SKUs.

What to check

Identify listings with outdated content, missing attributes, or inconsistent formatting across the catalog.

Corrective actions

Use bulk upload templates, standardise content structure, and set a regular review cycle for catalog hygiene.

When support helps

Ongoing catalog management is one of the areas Desiretech supports for sellers with larger, growing product ranges.
